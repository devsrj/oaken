The Facility

Advanced machinery, engineered precision.

Our 40,000 sq ft plant in Kakkodi runs the latest woodworking automation, so tolerances stay down to fractions of a millimeter and quality stays consistent across every run — from a single custom joint to a 50-apartment turnkey fit-out.

Cherukulam Road, Kakkodi, Kozhikode
German Technology

HOMAG Edge Banding

Seamless, zero-glue-line edge finishing with hot-melt PUR bonding. Delivers waterproof, heat-resistant edges that never peel in Kerala's coastal humidity.

Hydraulic Pressing

Hydraulic Hot Press

Evenly calibrated multi-ton hydraulic pressure ensures 100% void-free bonding of natural veneers and laminates, eliminating panel warping for life.

Precision Sizing

Precision Panel Saws

Industrial sliding beam saws with scoring blades deliver dead-square, micro-tolerance cuts without chip-out, guaranteeing crisp joins in every carcase.

CNC System 32

Multi-Boring & CNC

Automated European System 32 drilling and profiling. Every hinge hole, dowel point, and shelf pin aligns with mathematical precision without manual drilling errors.
